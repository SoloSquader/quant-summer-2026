Answer: 24.24%

## Problem 5

Question: A trader’s signal says “buy” or “sell.” On profitable days, the signal says “buy” 75% of the time. On unprofitable days, the signal says “buy” 40% of the time. Answer: 44.5%

Result: Correct.
